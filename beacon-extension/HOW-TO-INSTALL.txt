  BEACON — SALES NAVIGATOR BRIDGE
  Chrome extension · How to install & use
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
Pulls founder leads from LinkedIn Sales Navigator straight into
Beacon — no copy-pasting. Scan a lead profile or a search results
page, queue the interesting ones, then send them to Beacon in one
click (or download them as a CSV and drop the file onto Beacon).


INSTALL (one-time, ~1 minute)
-----------------------------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent (e.g. Documents).
   ⚠ Don't delete the folder afterwards — Chrome loads the
   extension from here every time it starts.

2. Open Chrome and go to:  chrome://extensions

3. Turn ON "Developer mode" — toggle in the top-right corner.

4. Click "Load unpacked" (top-left) and select the
   "beacon-extension" folder you just unzipped.

5. Done. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin
   "Beacon — Sales Navigator Bridge" so it's always visible.


FIRST-TIME SETUP
----------------
1. Click the Beacon icon in your toolbar.
2. Click "⚙ Beacon URL" at the bottom of the popup.
3. Paste the Beacon app URL:  https://enzo-beacon.vercel.app/
4. Click Save.


DAILY USE
---------
1. Browse LinkedIn Sales Navigator as usual.
2. On a lead profile or search results page, click the Beacon icon
   → "🔍 Scan page".
3. Untick anyone you don't want, then "Add selected to queue".
   Repeat on as many pages as you like — the queue keeps growing.
4. When you're done, click "Send to Beacon". Beacon opens (or
   refreshes) with the new leads imported and scored.

   Alternative: click "CSV" instead to download the queue as a
   file, then drag-drop that file anywhere onto the Beacon app.

TIP — On a full lead profile, the extension also reads the
person's work history. If they've already LEFT their last job
(no role marked "Present"), Beacon records the leave date and
shows "left last job Nd ago" — the strongest stealth signal.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
• "Could not scan" → refresh the LinkedIn tab, then scan again.
• "Could not reach Beacon tab" → open the Beacon app in a tab,
  let it fully load, then click "Send to Beacon" again.
• Leads didn't appear → check the Beacon URL in ⚙ settings
  matches the address in your browser exactly.
• After updating the extension (new zip), go to
  chrome://extensions and click the ↻ reload arrow on the
  Beacon card.
